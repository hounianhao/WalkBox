WalkBox 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-09-21 ・ 开发者 / Developer：WalkBox
核心承诺WalkBox 是一款离线优先、隐私优先的走路奖励应用。您的足迹、步数、宝箱等个人数据仅存储在您设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器，我们也不向任何第三方出售或共享您的个人数据。

1. 我们收集的数据

1.1 设备本地数据（不上传）

  • 足迹与步数：为提供走路记录与奖励，App 在本地记录您的步行轨迹、足迹点与步数。
  • 宝箱 / 记忆碎片：您拾取的宝箱与收集物品仅保存在本机数据库，用于应用内展示与奖励。
  • 上述数据全部仅存于设备本地，App 不发起任何将其上传至服务器的网络请求。

1.2 使用情况统计（仅埋点）

我们集成了一套第三方统计分析 SDK（Firebase Analytics，即 Google Analytics for Firebase），仅用于产品使用情况的匿名埋点统计（如功能点击、页面访问、崩溃与性能信息），以帮助改进产品体验。
  • 该 SDK 可能收集：设备型号、操作系统版本、应用版本、匿名设备标识及使用行为数据。
  • 这些数据仅用于埋点分析，不用于广告定向、不与其他数据关联识别个人身份。
  • 其数据处理受该服务商隐私政策约束：https://policies.google.com/privacy（Google 隐私权政策）。
  • 您可在 App「设置 → 匿名统计」中随时开启或关闭统计采集；调试版本默认关闭上报。

2. 数据如何使用

  • 本地数据：用于在 App 内展示足迹地图、回忆册、收集图鉴与发放走路奖励。
  • 埋点数据：用于了解功能使用情况、诊断崩溃、持续优化产品。除统计分析服务商外，不与任何第三方共享。

3. 数据共享与第三方

重要我们不出售您的任何数据，也不与广告商或数据经纪商共享。除第 1.2 节所述的统计分析服务商接收匿名埋点数据外，不存在其他任何数据共享。

4. 权限说明

  • 定位权限：用于记录步行轨迹与足迹点（仅本地使用）。
  • 运动 / 健身（活动识别）权限：用于计步与识别步行状态。
  • 通知权限：用于宝箱掉落等提醒。

您可随时在系统设置中撤回上述权限；撤回后相关功能可能受限，但不影响其他本地功能。

5. 数据存储与安全

  • 个人足迹与奖励数据仅存储于设备本地，不上传云端。
  • 我们采用系统级存储隔离保护本地数据；但请知悉任何设备存储都无法保证绝对安全，建议您妥善保管设备。

6. 您的权利

  • 您可在 App 内「设置 → 清除全部数据」一键删除所有本地足迹与奖励数据。
  • 卸载 App 即删除本地数据库。
  • 如对本政策有疑问，可联系：hounianhao@gmail.com

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童（或适用司法辖区等效年龄）设计，我们亦不有意收集儿童数据。如您认为我们误收集了儿童数据，请通过上述邮箱联系我们删除。

8. 政策变更

我们可能适时更新本政策，更新后将在本页面发布并标注生效日期。重大变更将通过 App 内公告告知。

Our CommitmentWalkBox is an offline-first, privacy-first walking rewards app. Your footprints, steps, and chests are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server. We do not sell or share your personal data with any third party.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 On-Device Data (Not Uploaded)

  • Footprints & Steps: To provide walking records and rewards, the app records your walking tracks, footprint points, and step counts locally.
  • Chests / Memory Fragments: Items you collect are stored only in the local database for in-app display and rewards.
  • All such data is stored locally on your device only; the app makes no network requests to upload it.

1.2 Usage Analytics (Analytics Only)

We integrate a third-party analytics SDK (Firebase Analytics, i.e. Google Analytics for Firebase) used solely for anonymous product analytics (e.g., feature clicks, screen views, crash and performance telemetry) to improve the product.
  • The SDK may collect: device model, OS version, app version, anonymous device identifiers, and usage behavior.
  • This data is used for analytics only and not for advertising targeting or re-identification.
  • Its data handling is governed by the provider's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy (Google Privacy Policy).
  • You can turn analytics collection on or off at any time in Settings → Anonymous Statistics; debug builds disable reporting by default.

2. How We Use Data

  • Local data: To display footprint maps, memory albums, collection gallery, and grant walking rewards within the app.
  • Analytics data: To understand feature usage, diagnose crashes, and improve the product. Shared with no third party other than the analytics provider.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ImportantWe do not sell any of your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Except for the anonymous analytics data received by the provider in Section 1.2, there is no other data sharing whatsoever.

4. Permissions

  • Location: To record walking tracks and footprint points (used locally only).
  • Activity / Fitness (Activity Recognition): To count steps and detect walking.
  • Notifications: For chest-drop and other reminders.

You may revoke these permissions anytime in system settings; doing so may limit related features but does not affect other local functions.

5. Storage & Security

  • Personal footprint and reward data is stored locally on your device only and not uploaded to the cloud.
  • We rely on OS-level storage isolation to protect local data; however, no device storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure—please keep your device safe.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete all local footprints and rewards in one tap via Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the local database.
  • For questions about this policy, contact: hounianhao@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe we inadvertently collected a child's data, contact us via the email above to request deletion.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be announced in-app.
